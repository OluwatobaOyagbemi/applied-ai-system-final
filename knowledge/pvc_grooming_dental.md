## Dental
- Periodontal (gum) disease causes most pet dental problems. Signs include bad
  breath, tartar buildup, loose teeth, appetite loss, and irritability.
- Use a pet dental product **daily** to slow tartar and gum infection.
- **Never use human toothpaste** on pets; it can be toxic if swallowed. Use a
  pet toothpaste. Most pets accept a toothbrush if introduced gradually.
- Hard food helps a little but does not replace brushing.
- Have teeth examined by a vet **at least twice a year**. Professional
  cleanings are done under sedation at the clinic when the vet recommends them;
  many pets over 5 need one every 6-12 months.

## Grooming
- Bathe a dog about **every 2 weeks** with a shampoo made for dog skin.
- Rinse a dog's feet after contact with road salt or ice-melt chemicals.
- A dull, dry, or thinning coat can signal illness and is worth a vet visit.
