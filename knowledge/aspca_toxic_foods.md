Paraphrased summary. General guidance, not veterinary advice.

## Never feed
- Chocolate, coffee and caffeine (darker chocolate and cocoa are worst).
- Xylitol (in gum, candy, baked goods, some toothpaste): can cause low blood
  sugar and liver damage within 12-24 hours.
- Grapes and raisins: kidney damage in dogs.
- Onions, garlic and chives: damage red blood cells and cause anemia; cats are
  more sensitive, dogs are at risk too.
- Alcohol: vomiting, tremors, coma, death.
- Raw yeast dough: can expand and twist the stomach, a life-threatening
  emergency.
- Macadamia nuts: weakness, wobbling and vomiting in dogs, usually within 12
  hours.
- Also avoid: avocado, citrus, coconut, other nuts (fat can cause
  pancreatitis), very salty foods, dairy (diarrhea), raw meat and eggs
  (bacteria), and bones (cooked bones splinter; raw bones can need surgery).

## If a pet eats one
Note what and how much, then contact the vet or the ASPCA Animal Poison Control
Center at (888) 426-4435. Don't wait for signs.
