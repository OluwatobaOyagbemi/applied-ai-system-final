Paraphrased summary. General guidance, not veterinary advice. The original page
is promotional (it names specific food brands); brand claims are omitted.

- Dogs and cats are primarily meat-eaters; animal protein is the most
  digestible protein for them.
- A complete, balanced, consistent diet avoids stomach upset that can come with
  frequent formula changes.
- Young animals have small stomachs and need nutrient-dense food.
- Follow the feeding chart on the label and your vet's advice on portions;
  discuss nutrition with your vet, especially if weight changes.
- Avoid table scraps.
