Paraphrased summary. General guidance, not veterinary advice.

## Water and food
Keep fresh water available at all times; wash and refill the bowl daily. Treats
should be no more than 5-10% of the diet. Ask the vet about diet and portions.

## Litter box
Scoop solid waste at least once a day. Empty, wash and refill the box at least
once a week, using mild soap or hot water, not ammonia, deodorants or strong
scents (especially lemon). Provide one box per cat plus one extra. Kittens and
older cats need boxes with low sides.

## Grooming and scratching
Trim nails every two to three weeks. Provide a scratching post at least three
feet tall.

## Enrichment
Indoor cats need enrichment for physical and mental health: daily play, puzzle
feeders or hidden food portions, scratching posts and vertical space.

## Vet care and safety
A vet exam at least once a year, and right away if the cat is sick or injured.
Call the vet if loss of appetite, vomiting, diarrhea or lethargy lasts more than
two days. A male cat straining to urinate is an emergency. Keep cats indoors.
Never give a cat medication a vet didn't prescribe. Never pick a cat up by the
scruff or the front legs.
