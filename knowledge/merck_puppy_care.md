## Feeding schedule
- 6-12 weeks: 4 meals a day
- 3-6 months: 3 meals a day
- 6-12 months: 2 meals a day
- Over 12 months: 1 or 2 adult meals a day
Choose a food labelled complete and balanced for growth (AAFCO statement).

## Growing up
Most dogs are adult at 9-12 months; large and giant breeds can take almost two
years.

## Vet visits and vaccines
Puppies get vaccines every 2-3 weeks until about 4 months old and should avoid
unvaccinated dogs until then. The vet checks stool samples every 2-4 weeks until
two tests in a row are negative. The schedule itself is the vet's decision.

## Socialization
Socialization matters most between 2 and 4 months of age. Puppy classes may take
vaccinated puppies from about 8 weeks.
