Paraphrased summary. General guidance, not veterinary advice.

## By life stage
- Kittens up to 6 months: three meals a day.
- 6 months to 1 year: twice a day.
- Adults (from about 1 year): once or twice a day.
- Seniors (10 years and older): keep the same routine unless the vet says
  otherwise.

## Practical points
Food left out for grazing should be replaced every day. Wet food is about 70-80%
water. A cat is considered obese at 20% or more above normal weight. Never put a
cat on a crash diet: underfeeding can trigger hepatic lipidosis, a potentially
life-threatening liver condition. Diet changes for diabetes or kidney disease
are made with the vet.
