Paraphrased summary. General guidance, not veterinary advice.

## How often
Daily brushing is the goal (AAHA and VOHC both recommend daily). VCA gives three
times a week as the minimum that still helps remove plaque and slow tartar.

## How
About 30 seconds per side of the mouth. Start while the pet is young and build
up slowly with praise. Replace the toothbrush every three months. Wash hands
afterwards.

## Never
Never use human toothpaste: it can contain xylitol, which is toxic to dogs, plus
detergents and fluoride. Baking soda is not recommended either.

## Why it matters
More than 80% of dogs over age three have periodontal disease. Brushing doesn't
replace vet dental exams and professional cleanings, which VCA recommends at
least once a year for most pets. Dental chews can help when brushing isn't
possible, but brushing works best.
