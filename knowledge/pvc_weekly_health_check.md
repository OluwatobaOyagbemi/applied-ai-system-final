Paraphrased summary. General guidance, not veterinary advice.

The clinic asks owners to examine their pet once a week and run through a
yes/no checklist. If any answer is "no", the owner should call the vet for an
appointment. The checklist covers:

- Behavior: acting normal, active, in good spirits.
- Appetite: normal, no trouble chewing or swallowing.
- Breathing: normal, no straining.
- Urine and bowel movements: usual amount, frequency, and appearance.
- Movement: walks without stiffness, pain, or difficulty.
- Coat and skin: coat full and glossy; skin not flaky or greasy.
- Parasites: no fleas, ticks, lice, or mites.
- Eyes bright and clear; ears clean inside; nose moist, no discharge.
- Teeth clean and free of tartar; gums pink, not red.

A planner should treat this as a recurring weekly task of about 10 minutes and
should tell the owner to contact their vet if any check fails, rather than
suggesting a diagnosis or treatment.
