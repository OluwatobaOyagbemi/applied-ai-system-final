Paraphrased summary. General guidance, not veterinary advice.

## Never give or leave within reach
- **Chocolate** (dogs): small amounts can cause illness or death.
- **Antifreeze**: tastes sweet to dogs and is fatal if swallowed.
- **Human medications**: even small doses of acetaminophen (Tylenol), aspirin,
  diet pills, sleeping pills, or tranquilizers can be fatal. **Never give a pet
  any medication, including over-the-counter products, without asking a vet.**
- Cleaning products, bleach, detergent, paint and thinner, pesticides,
  fertilizer, mothballs, rodent and roach poison: store in closed cabinets.

## Poisonous plants named in the source
Amaryllis, English ivy, narcissus, dieffenbachia, mistletoe, poinsettia, holly,
philodendron, azalea, rhododendron, daffodil, daphne, foxglove, bleeding heart,
potato plant, iris, oleander, rubber plant, tobacco, tulip, clematis, morning
glory, weeping fig.

## Pet-proofing for puppies and kittens
- Screen windows; keep pets off balconies and high decks.
- Keep toilet lids down; store plastic bags; cut apart six-pack rings.
- Shorten or secure electrical cords.
- Keep sewing supplies, nails, staples, and small hardware out of reach.
- Check where the pet is before closing the fridge, oven, washer, or dryer.
- Use only a proper collar, never ribbons.
- Never leave a pet unattended in a car.
- Keep dogs fenced and cats indoors to avoid getting lost or hit by cars.
