Paraphrased summary. General guidance, not veterinary advice.

- **Water:** keep fresh water available at all times and change it daily.
- **Walks:** walk or jog your dog every day. (The brochure does not give a
  length; see `akc_dog_exercise.md` for durations by age and breed.)
- **Play:** play with your dog daily, and use that time to watch for signs of
  illness.
- **Litter boxes:** clean cat litter boxes daily and wash hands afterwards.
  Pregnant people should avoid cleaning the litter box (toxoplasmosis risk), or
  at least wear gloves.
- **Yard:** remove pet droppings from the yard at least 2-3 times a week;
  daily is best.
- **Hygiene:** wash hands after handling soil or playing with pets.
- **Feeding habits:** avoid table scraps; never give a dog bones that can
  splinter or be swallowed.
- **Weight:** monitor your dog's weight; if you can't feel the rib cage, the dog
  is likely overweight. Discuss nutrition and portions with your vet.
- **Training:** start obedience training young; praise works better than
  punishment.
