## Who counts as senior
Dogs older than about 7 and cats older than about 10.

## Vet visits
Exams every six months, even when the pet seems healthy (AVMA: at least twice a
year). Visits usually include a physical and dental check, and often bloodwork,
urinalysis and blood pressure.

## Mobility and comfort
Arthritis is one of the most common problems in older pets, and early treatment
helps. At home: non-slip flooring, ramps or pet stairs, orthopedic bedding,
low-sided litter boxes and raised bowls. Keep exercise low-impact and adjust it
to the pet's energy.

## Watch for and report
Trouble with stairs, circling a long time before lying down, or changes in how
the pet walks or jumps. A short video of the pet walking helps the vet spot
problems early.
